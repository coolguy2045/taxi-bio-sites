About

About Mr Ng

Aaron Ng Hoi-shan (伍海山) is a second-generation industry figure often referred to in Hong Kong media as a “taxi king.” His father, Ng Fong (伍熀)—founder of Tai Wo Motors—has also been associated with that nickname. Reports state that after inheriting or taking over the family business, Mr Ng expanded the fleet, including through bank financing.

Confirmed identity

  • Chinese name: 伍海山
  • English name: Aaron Ng Hoi-shan / NG Hoi Shan, Aaron
  • Honours: Medal of Honour (MH, 2023); Chief Executive’s Commendation for Community Service (2017)
  • Media-reported titles (Tai Wo Management): Executive Director / 常務董事 (Wen Wei Po); Managing Director (SCMP, The Standard, YAS)
  • Sporting role: President, Fencing Association of Hong Kong, China (per event and association materials)

Family and business succession

  • Father Ng Fong (伍熀): founder of Tai Wo Motors; began in To Kwa Wan selling car batteries; formerly chaired the Hong Kong Taxi and Public Light Bus Association; reports say the motor business was later entrusted to Aaron Ng.
  • Reports state Ng Fong has three sons; one son, Ng Fu-shan (伍富山), is an orthodontist.

Business

Career: Tai Wo Motors to JOIE

From Tai Wo Motors—described in Sing Tao–group coverage as rooted in Hong Kong for about half a century—to the Transport Department–licensed urban taxi fleet JOIE under Tai Wo Management, the focus remains quality taxi service and fleet operations.

Tai Wo Motors / Tai Wo Management

  • Tai Wo Motors Limited: reports and company records indicate establishment around 1984; address G/F, 15–19 Ha Heung Road, To Kwa Wan, Kowloon.
  • Sing Tao–group reporting has described Tai Wo Motors as rooted in Hong Kong for about 50 years.
  • Tai Wo Management Limited is the licensed operator of the JOIE fleet; media refer to Mr Ng as Executive Director or Managing Director.

Industry-related roles

  • Transport Department annual digests (approx. 2020–2023) listed him as Chairman of Hong Kong Taxi Call Centre Limited.
  • In June 2024, reports said his moderate slate won 14 of 15 council seats in the Hong Kong Taxi and Public Light Bus Association election; under association rules he was not immediately eligible for chair.

Historical newspaper figures (not current verified counts): In 2012, Wen Wei Po cited sources saying the largest taxi-licence holder was Tai Wo’s Aaron Ng—then reported as 611 urban and 187 New Territories licences—and that he expanded holdings by about 80% after buying licences heavily during the 2008 financial crisis. These figures reflect contemporaneous reporting only; no equivalent authoritative public update of current personal holdings has been identified.

JOIE taxi fleet

JOIE is an orange urban taxi fleet operated by Tai Wo Management Limited. On 28 July 2025, the Transport Department announced formal taxi-fleet licences; JOIE holds licence number 005 for five years and entered formal service that day.
