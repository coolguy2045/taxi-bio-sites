Cai Wensheng

蔡文胜 · also known as Mike Cai

About Cai Wensheng

Outreach

How to get noticed / seek investment

Practical guidance inferred from public interviews, published submission channels, and media patterns. It is advisory inference — not insider tips, guarantees, or scripts. Nothing here is an offer of securities or virtual assets.

!
Read first: Outcomes depend on the venture, compliance, and networks. Sector fit still means most pitches are declined. This site does not publish private WeChat, phone numbers, or home addresses; property reporting is not an invitation to visit.

  1. Sector self-check (public preferences)

    AI, Web3 / digital assets, Crypto×AI, or sustainable Web3 games / creator economy? Far-off sectors may see lower cold-email hit rates.

  2. Real traction & honest metrics

    Public selection logic favours doers and real users/metrics, and dislikes inflation. Bring verifiable demos and numbers — not empty decks.

  3. Official public channel

    Longling’s site invites business plans to:

    space@longling.com

    Public address: Nos. 58 Electric Road, Tin Hau, Hong Kong. CAI Tower / garage-café is a media-described ecosystem path for teams already in (or able to stay in) Hong Kong — still defer to official channels.

  4. Warm introductions (public pattern)

    Early interviews often cited peer referrals. Use lawful public networks (industry events, angel forums, open university talks). No private contact list is provided here.

  5. Listed-company IR is not a back door

    CAI Corp, Long Corp, and Meitu IR (e.g. ir@meitu.com) are for investor relations / compliance — not a shortcut to a personal angel meeting.

  6. Materials checklist (practical inference)

    • One-page EN/ZH summary: problem, product, traction, team, use of funds
    • Verifiable metrics or demo; clear cap table and round expectations
    • If virtual assets are involved: show basic awareness of Hong Kong’s regulatory frame; avoid unlawful promises
